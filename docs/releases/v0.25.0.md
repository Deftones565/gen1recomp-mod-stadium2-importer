# Battles look and move like Stadium 2

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.24.1...v0.25.0

No reimport needed.

# Changes

## Camera and battle flow

- **The hit lands when the camera cuts to the target.** A move's hit
  effect and the target's hit reaction now wait for the camera to reach
  the Pokemon that was hit, as in Stadium 2, each hit of a multi-hit move
  too.
- **A short shot of the Pokemon about to act** now plays between the two
  Pokemon's actions in a turn, as in Stadium 2.
- **Shots of one Pokemon hide the other,** as Stadium 2 does; a Pokemon
  appears when its send-out starts and stays out of sight underground.
- **"Fast asleep", "frozen solid" and similar shots** last Stadium 2's
  length.
- **Seismic Toss rolls the camera** upside down, and other rolling shots
  now roll on screen.

## Pokemon motion (Battle FX option)

- **Every per-move motion from Stadium 2:** Surf (riding and tilting with
  the wave), Waterfall, Submission, Rapid Spin, Faint Attack, Withdraw,
  Belly Drum, Destiny Bond, and the squash of a Stomp or Body Slam target.
  Poses now hold until the next camera shot, as in Stadium 2.
- **Fly:** the Pokemon rises into the air and stays up until its attack.
  **Dig:** it spins and sinks into the ground (Diglett and Dugtrio stay in
  their hole).
- **Moves a species has no animation for** (Hidden Power, and many others)
  no longer play a stand-in attack; the Pokemon keeps its pose, as in
  Stadium 2.

## Move effects (BETA)

- **Sleep and freeze:** the Z's and the ice now appear when a move puts a
  Pokemon to sleep (it plays its falling-asleep animation) or freezes it,
  when it uses Rest, and when it is sent out asleep or frozen. They last
  as long as the status, hide with the Pokemon, and go when it wakes up or
  thaws.
- **Sky Attack and Rollout:** the user comes back when the next move
  starts, instead of staying invisible for the rest of the turn.
- **Effects that hold their last frame** (Baton Pass and others) now hold
  instead of vanishing.
