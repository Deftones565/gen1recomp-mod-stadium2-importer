# Battle FX fixes

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.15.0...v0.15.1

Fixes for the **MOVE EFFECTS (BETA)** option (was *BETA STADIUM 2 BATTLE
FX*). No reimport needed.

# Fixes

- Gen 2: move effects now play. Previously only the Poke Ball send-out
  appeared
- Gen 1: move effects, burn/poison/Leech Seed effects and two-turn charge
  effects now play for the whole battle, not just before the first move
- With move effects on, the Game Boy move animations are hidden so only
  Stadium's effects show. Ball throws and catch shakes stay, since Stadium
  has no ball-throw effect
- Gen 2: the Game Boy battle screen no longer flashes up when a battle
  ends. The Stadium scene stays until the exit fade finishes and fades to
  white with the rest of the screen
- Much less lag on particle-heavy effects such as the Poke Ball send-out,
  Bubblebeam and Mimic

# Changes

- Shorter option names:
  - STADIUM 2 MODELS -> 3D POKEMON MODELS
  - STADIUM 2 BATTLE -> 3D BATTLE SCENE
  - STADIUM 2 BATTLE HUD -> BATTLE HUD
  - MODEL / SCENE SHADER -> SHADER STYLE
  - BETA STADIUM 2 BATTLE FX -> MOVE EFFECTS (BETA)
  - BETA CONTEXT ARENAS -> CONTEXT ARENAS (BETA)
  - BETA PARK TIME OF DAY -> PARK TIME OF DAY (BETA)

  Your saved settings are kept.

# Notes

- Move effects start from the game's own move animations, so keep the
  game's battle animation option on
- If a playthrough's Stadium cache is from an older version, the mod
  rebuilds it the first time you load that save

# Known Issues

- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet, and
  some effects may be missing, mistimed or look different
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium's camera moves are not in yet; battles keep the standard camera
- Stadium sounds are not played
