# Move effects: no more white squares on hits, and effects that end on time

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.20.0...v0.21.0

No reimport needed. These changes are to **MOVE EFFECTS (BETA)**.

# Changes

- **Fixed: a white square on hits.** Pound, the punches and kicks, Tackle,
  Slash and about 100 other moves flashed a large white square where the
  hit landed. Their small hit sparks were drawn at full size for one frame
  as each one appeared. Effects are now drawn only after they are set up,
  as in Stadium 2.
- **Effects end on time:**
  - Delayed parts of a move fire on time and in order, even when the game
    catches up after a slow frame.
  - A newer move, or a Pokemon being switched out, cancels hits still
    waiting from the move before.
  - Finished effects are cleared away, so long battles no longer slow down.
  - Long-lasting effects (Mist, Haze, Rain Dance, the powders) end by
    Stadium 2's own rule.
- **Vice Grip, Guillotine, Bite and similar "jaws":** they leave when the
  hit lands instead of staying on screen. (Stadium 2 cuts its camera to the
  target there; this game has a free camera, so they are hidden instead.)
- **Bind and Wrap:** the end-of-turn effect on the trapped Pokemon now plays.
- **Gen 2 battles:** Curse (Ghost-type and stat versions), Snore, Thief and
  Present pick the right version of their effect, and a shiny Pokemon's
  send-out plays its shiny effect.

# Known Issues

- Slashing effects (Scratch, Slash, Cut and others) can stay on screen after
  they play.
- Shiny send-out effects are not yet shown in Gen 1 battles.
