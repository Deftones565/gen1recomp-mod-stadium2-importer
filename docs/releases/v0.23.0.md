# The Stadium 2 camera, complete

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.22.0...v0.23.0

No reimport needed. Set **CAMERA** to **STADIUM** to use it.

# Changes

- **The Stadium camera is complete.** Every camera moment Stadium 2 has
  that this game can trigger is now ported from the game's own code.
- **Hits:** the camera shakes right after the impact, harder for stronger
  hits (not very effective, normal, super effective, critical). It then
  holds on the hit Pokemon until its hit animation ends, or shortly after
  the HP bar stops. This now works with MOVE EFFECTS off too; before, hits
  had no camera at all without it.
- **Each turn:** after the turn-start sweep, the camera turns to the
  Pokemon that moves first, as Stadium 2 does.
- **Waking up:** the camera holds on the Pokemon until it has woken up.
  Fixed: this close-up aimed far off the arena for many Pokemon (for
  example Blastoise, Gengar and Lugia).
- **Special moves:** Foresight re-aims during the move. Lock-On aims at its
  effect (with MOVE EFFECTS on). Multi-hit moves hold for a shorter time
  between hits (Gen 2 with MOVE EFFECTS off).
- **Switching:** Roar and Whirlwind in Gen 2 use the right camera for the
  dragged-in Pokemon's status. Gen 1 trainers now get the recall camera
  when they switch Pokemon.
- **Stability:** the move-effect code no longer needs a library the game
  refuses to mods while running. That was the cause of the earlier idle
  camera flicker.

# Known Issues

- Your own switch in Gen 2 has no recall camera: the game has no
  "come back" step to attach it to.
- The camera has not been checked in every battle situation yet.
