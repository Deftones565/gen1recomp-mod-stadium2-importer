# Battle FX performance

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.15.1...v0.15.2

Big performance improvement for **MOVE EFFECTS (BETA)**, aimed at phones.
Effects look the same as before. No reimport needed.

# Changes

- Identical particles are now drawn together in one draw call. The Poke
  Ball send-out went from about 145 extra draw calls per frame to 1-2
- Much less per-frame work and memory churn for particles: effect update
  and draw time at the busiest moment of the send-out dropped by about 5x
- Particle-heavy effects such as the Poke Ball send-out, Bubblebeam and
  Mimic should no longer lag on phones

# Known Issues

- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet, and
  some effects may be missing, mistimed or look different
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium's camera moves are not in yet; battles keep the standard camera
- Stadium sounds are not played
