# EXTRA EFFECTS option for phones

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.15.3...v0.15.4

Aimed at phones. No reimport needed.

# Changes

- **EXTRA EFFECTS** (ON / LITE / OFF, under GRAPHICS) replaces PARTICLES.
  Your saved setting carries over
  - **ON**: everything, as before
  - **LITE**: move effects still play, but big particle bursts (sparkles,
    trails, bubbles, the Poke Ball burst) draw about half their particles.
    Single objects such as projectiles and the Poke Ball always show. Also
    turns off shadows in the Kenney scenes, rain and thunderstorms, and
    ambient Pokemon
  - **OFF**: like LITE, and hides all move-effect particles
  - Move timing is unchanged in every mode. Stadium arenas keep their
    shadows. This is a mod option, not something the original game has
- LITE halves the particles drawn in heavy effects (Poke Ball send-out,
  Bubblebeam, Mimic) and cuts their draw time by about a third

# Known Issues

- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet, and
  some effects may be missing, mistimed or look different
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium's camera moves are not in yet; battles keep the standard camera
- Stadium sounds are not played
