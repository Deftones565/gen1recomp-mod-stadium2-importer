# Eleven new painted battle scenes

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.15.6...v0.16.0

With **BATTLE ENVIRONMENT** set to **KENNEY NATURE**, every kind of location
now has its own painted watercolor scene. No reimport needed.

# New scenes

- **Ocean**: both Pokemon ride log rafts on a painted sea, with a palm
  island and pier, sea stacks, islets and boats on the horizon. At night:
  stars, a moon and a silver moon path on the water
- **Mountain**: a meadow ledge above a hazy valley and snow-capped peaks
- **Ice Cave**: icy floor, icicles, frozen pillars and glowing crystals
- **Cave Water**: an underground lake with rafts and glowing crystals
- **Indoor Water**: a bright tiled pool hall with floating platforms
- **Interior**: a cozy home with furniture, rug and sunny windows
- **Industrial**: a factory floor with tanks, conveyors, crane and catwalk
- **Ruins**: a worn stone court with broken columns, arch and lanterns
- **Ship**: the deck of a tall ship at sea
- **Gym**: a battle court with stands, banners and statues
- **League**: a grand hall with a red carpet, columns and braziers

Outdoor scenes have rain, thunderstorms and ambient Pokemon; outdoor scenes
also get a starry night sky. Props come from Kenney 3D kits (CC0), repainted
to match the other watercolor scenes.

# Changes

- Test room: all the new scenes are in its scene list
- TEST ENVIRONMENT no longer marks these scenes as fallbacks

# Known Issues

- The new scenes have not been played on a phone yet; report any lag or
  anything that looks wrong
- Some effects can look turned relative to the Pokemon in Classic and
  Kenney scenes; still being investigated
- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium sounds are not played
