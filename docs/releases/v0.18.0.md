# Evolution in the 3D battle; HD Stadium UI

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.17.1...v0.18.0

No reimport needed.

# Changes

- **Evolution in battle (new, a mod addition):** a Pokemon that evolves at
  the end of a won battle evolves in the 3D battle instead of on the game's
  evolution screen:
  - the camera closes in and the field dims
  - the Pokemon turns white and its old and new models trade places on the
    game's own flash beats, with sparkles rising around it
  - the white fades off the new form with a burst of light
  - the game's texts show in the Stadium message box
  - a Pokemon that is not the one out is sent out first; B still cancels
  - if a model can't load, the game's own evolution screen is used
- **UI DETAIL option (new):** `HD` (default) smooths the Stadium UI's art
  and font and redraws the N64 button icons sharply; `N64 PIXELS` keeps the
  original pixels
- **Fixed (Gen 2):** with another mod's battle UI (such as Stadium 2 UI), a
  white Game Boy screen covered the middle of the 3D battle
- Works with Stadium 2 UI 1.0.2, which stands aside while the evolution
  plays

# Known Issues

- The in-battle evolution is new and has not been checked in every case
  (Gen 1 and Gen 2, cancelled, a Pokemon that was not out)
- Stadium UI: it matches the decomp and ROM data but has not been compared
  with the real game on every screen
- Portrait lighting is not decoded yet; a neutral light is used
- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet
- Stadium sounds are not played
