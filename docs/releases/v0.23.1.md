# Strike marks no longer stay on screen

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.23.0...v0.23.1

No reimport needed. These changes are to **MOVE EFFECTS (BETA)**.

# Changes

- **Fixed: strike marks stayed on screen** for about 8 seconds after moves
  like Scratch, Cut, Wing Attack, Vine Whip, Fury Swipes, Slash, False
  Swipe, Fury Cutter, Steel Wing, Rapid Spin, Iron Tail, Metal Claw and
  Cross Chop. Stadium 2 clears leftover effects when the battle returns to
  the command menu after a turn, and when a Pokemon is sent out. The mod
  now does the same, with every camera setting.
- Screen overlays such as Mist, the powders, Smokescreen, Haze and
  Sandstorm now also end at that point, as in Stadium 2.

# Known Issues

- Stadium also clears effects at the start of status and residual events;
  that part is not wired yet.
