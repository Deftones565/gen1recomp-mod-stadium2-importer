# Battles play one event at a time

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.23.1...v0.24.0

No reimport needed.

# Changes

## Battle flow and camera

- **Events no longer overlap.** As in Stadium 2, the next battle event now
  waits until the current one has finished: send-outs, switches, attacks,
  hits, faints, status effects and the turn-start shot. The camera always
  has one thing to show, and the foe's move no longer plays while your
  Pokemon is still coming out or attacking. Gen 1 and Gen 2, with either
  camera option. No wait lasts more than 8 seconds.
- **The camera stays on the attacker** until its move has finished, then
  cuts to the Pokemon that was hit.
- **Pokemon shadows** no longer appear on the floor before the Pokemon has
  come out of its Poke Ball.

## Move effects (BETA)

- **Absorb, Mega Drain, Leech Life, Giga Drain, Dream Eater:** the drain
  into the user now plays when its HP rises (it never played in Gen 1).
- **Effects that looked frozen now move:** many move effects (Absorb and
  its family, Gust, Whirlwind, Roar, Razor Wind, Meditate, Focus Energy,
  Rage, Bide...) scroll their textures again, as in Stadium 2.
- **Meditate** stretches the user, as in Stadium 2.
- **Stat changes in Gen 1** ("ATTACK rose!", "RAGE is building!") now
  play Stadium's stat effects.
- **Whirlwind and Roar (Gen 2):** the dragged-in Pokemon fades in with a
  flash, and the wind no longer stays on screen.
- **Strike marks** (Scratch, Slash, Vine Whip...) are also cleared when a
  status effect starts, as in Stadium 2.

## Extensions (not in Stadium 2, added on request)

- When a move has no effect, "<Pokemon> used <MOVE>!" waits for A/B before
  "It doesn't affect ..." appears.
- A move that misses or has no effect still shows its attempt: the
  attacker plays the move, then the target dodges.

## Mobile

- Fixed broken faces on some models on phones (for example Koffing).

# Known Issues

- The hit effect of a move still plays a little before the camera cuts to
  the Pokemon that was hit; the fix is next.
- Some per-move Pokemon motions (Surf, Seismic Toss, Rapid Spin and a few
  others) are not in yet.
