# New

- **Test room**: open it from **FX TEST ROOM** in the game's OPTIONS menu
  or **TEST ROOM** in the mod's settings. It uses the same battle scene
  and move effects as real battles, and your graphics settings apply
  - Play any move (1-251) or battle effect (252-301), such as the Poke
    Ball send-out
  - Pick either Pokemon, swap who attacks, and switch between the Classic,
    Woodland, Cave, Freshwater and Town scenes and DAY / EVE / NITE
  - Drag to turn the camera, pinch or scroll to zoom. PAUSE and HIDE
    (hides the controls) are in the top bar, next to an FPS counter
  - Works with touch, mouse, keyboard and gamepad. The controls scale to
    the screen and rearrange for portrait and landscape
  - EXIT, the phone's back button, Escape or gamepad B returns to the game
  - A mod tool, not part of Stadium 2
- Each battle with move effects on now writes one log line with the mod
  version and the EXTRA EFFECTS and POKE BALL settings in use

# Known Issues

  some effects may be missing, mistimed or look different
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium's camera moves are not in yet; battles keep the standard camera
- Stadium sounds are not played
