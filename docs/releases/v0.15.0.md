# Stadium 2 Battle FX (Beta)

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.14.5...v0.15.0

New option: **BETA STADIUM 2 BATTLE FX** (off by default). Turn it on to
replace the Game Boy move animations with Stadium 2's own battle effects,
decoded from your imported ROM. Works in Gen 1 and Gen 2 battles. Takes
effect from the next battle.

**Reimport required:** the cache format changed, so the mod will ask you
to import your Stadium 2 ROM again.

# Battle effects

- Move effects from Stadium 2, played on Stadium's timing: the effect
  starts when the Pokemon strikes and the hit lands on the defender at the
  moment Stadium plays it
- The defender plays its hit reaction at the impact
- Two-turn moves (Razor Wind, Fly, SolarBeam, Dig, Skull Bash, Sky Attack)
  show Stadium's charge turn
- Agility, Double Team, Minimize and Rest use Stadium's own Pokemon
  routines (sway and afterimages, clones, shrinking, sleep)
- Battle events: poison, burn, Leech Seed, Curse, Nightmare, stat raises
  and drops, draining moves, berries, send-out, faint, withdrawing a
  Pokemon, and Bind/Wrap/Fire Spin/Clamp/Whirlpool damage
- Gen 2 weather: rain, sun and sandstorm effects each turn and when they end
- Sleep, frozen, Fly and Dig poses

# Fixes

- Textures fixed on Swords Dance, wind and streak effects, Razor Leaf and
  Petal Dance
- Sandstorm no longer draws as a black screen
- Removed the lines drawn through full-screen effects
- Big performance improvement on particle-heavy moves

# Known Issues

- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet, and
  some effects may be missing, mistimed or look different
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium's camera moves are not in yet; battles keep the standard camera
- Stadium sounds are not played
