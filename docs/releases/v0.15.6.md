# Stadium arenas in the test room

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.15.5...v0.15.6

No reimport needed.

# Changes

- Test room: the scene selector now also lists every Stadium arena (ARENA
  00 FALKNER onward), after Classic and the Kenney scenes. Arenas load
  from the Stadium 2 ROM your game imported; if one cannot load, the room
  says why and shows Classic
- Classic and Kenney scenes: move effects are now worked out with the
  Pokemon placed the way Stadium places them, then drawn in the scene.
  Some Stadium effect routines assume that layout

# Known Issues

- Some effects can look turned or sideways relative to the Pokemon in
  Classic and Kenney scenes; still being investigated
- Beta: many moves have not been checked against the real game yet, and
  some effects may be missing, mistimed or look different
- Hydro Pump's water is not visible
- Slowpoke and Slowbro have no hit reaction
- Stadium's camera moves are not in yet; battles keep the standard camera
- Stadium sounds are not played
