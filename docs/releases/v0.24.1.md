# Pokemon stand (and float) where Stadium 2 puts them

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.24.0...v0.24.1

No reimport needed.

# Changes

- **Flying and floating Pokemon float again.** Stadium 2 places every
  Pokemon at a height from its own data; the mod stood them all on the
  floor. Fearow, Gastly, Haunter, Koffing, Butterfree, Zubat, Magnemite
  and many others now hover at Stadium's height, and the STADIUM camera's
  shots, which already aimed there, frame them properly.
- **Steelix** now stands at its own spot on the field, as in Stadium 2,
  instead of in the middle.

# Known Issues

- A move's hit effect still plays before the camera cuts to the Pokemon
