# The Stadium 2 battle camera

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/Deftones565/gen1recomp-mod-stadium2-importer/compare/v0.21.0...v0.22.0

No reimport needed. Set **CAMERA** to **STADIUM** to use the new camera.

# Changes

- **New: CAMERA STADIUM follows Stadium 2's own battle camera.** It is
  ported from the game's camera code. It covers:
  - attacks, hits, dodges, faints, send-outs and recalls;
  - status and weather, charge turns (Fly, Dig and others), confusion,
    Substitute, Transform, Beat Up and being dragged out;
  - the victory shot.
- **The battle opening:** the split-screen arena intro, then the send-out
  wipe to the foe. Each Pokemon's entrance animation plays when the camera
  shows it, as in Stadium 2.
- **The command menu's idle camera:** the slow orbits, the close-ups and the
  over-the-shoulder shot, while the game waits for your command.
- **Wild battles** get their own opening: a close-up of the wild Pokemon,
  then an arena orbit. Stadium 2 has no wild battles, so this part is new
  and not from the original game.
- **Custom scenes:** the Stadium camera also works on the painted scenes.
  There the Pokemon use Stadium 2's proportions, so they look smaller.
- **Move effects:** more effects run Stadium 2's own code.

# Known Issues

- Some camera moments are not in yet: the shake after a hit, the end of
  the turn, Lock-On and Foresight.
- The camera has not been checked in every battle situation yet.
